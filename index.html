Design intent

Fanr Homes is not a classifieds site — it is a data utility where every property has a page, a price estimate (تقدير فَنر), and a legal status. The design leads with the two assets no competitor owns: the Riyadh Legal Rent Indicator (مؤشر الإيجار النظامي) and verification depth (REGA ad licence + FAL + Nafath surfaced as first-class UI). Arabic-first RTL throughout; the Foreign Investor module is English-first. Homes ships as a module inside the existing Fanr app — one brand, one bottom nav, with cross-sell into Cost Estimator, Providers, Developer Mode and AI-PMO.

Design principles

1 · Legal truth above market opinion

In Riyadh the lawful rent is a fact, not a negotiation. Wherever both numbers exist, the legal cap governs the layout: it is the primary number, the market estimate is context (PRD §7.3, E5.1).

2 · A range, never a bare number

تقدير فَنر always renders as range + point + confidence label, with the Taqeem disclaimer in UI copy — «مؤشر سوقي استرشادي وليس تقييماً عقارياً معتمداً» (R3.5.1, R-E4-2, R-E4-7).

3 · Verification as visible trust

Ad-licence number, FAL licence and Nafath identity are rendered as a badge stack on every listing — compliance becomes the brand (R3.1.3, E13).

4 · The listing is the start, not the end

Every property page carries the journey forward: renovation cost (pre-filled Cost Estimator), providers, financing, Ejar readiness (R-E3-4, E6).
