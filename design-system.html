6 · Layout, RTL & accessibility rules

RuleSpec
Grid & spacing4px base; screen padding 15px; card radius 18px, controls 13px; bottom nav 5 tabs
RTLArabic is the primary direction — mirrored icons, chevrons point left (‹), progress flows right→left. Foreign-investor module is LTR English; digits always LTR
Touch targets≥44×44pt; primary CTA full-width and bottom-anchored on flows
ContrastText ≥4.5:1; badges pair colour+icon+label; focus rings 2px #2B4FD8
EstimatesNever a bare number: range + confidence + disclaimer wherever an estimate renders (R-E4-2/7). No estimate at LOW confidence with 0 comps — show «بيانات غير كافية لهذا الحي»
PhotographyReal listing media only; gradient placeholders in this package stand in for photos/3D tours
